\section{Quick Reference: Sprint Checklist}
%=============================================================================

The following table provides a high-level overview of deliverables by sprint. Always refer to the official project document and Canvas for exact submission requirements and deadlines.

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{} c c l X @{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Sprint} & \textbf{Weight} & \textbf{Focus} & \textbf{Key Deliverables} \\
\midrule
1--3 & 20\% & Market Analysis, SDRD, PDR, CDR & Market analysis, patent search, AI persona interviews, SDRD, test plan, reverse engineering, FMEA, PDR/CDR reports, manufacturing review, purchase requests, retrospectives \\
4 & 20\% & Critical Subsystem Demos & Demo proposal (before sprint), demo videos, demo report, retrospective \\
5 & 20\% & Complete System Demo & System demo report (state, BoM, updated docs, revised test plan), in-class demo, retrospective \\
6 & 10\% & External Team Testing & External testing report (performed by another team) \\
7 & --- & Refinement and Retesting & No formal submissions; fix issues, prepare final presentation and report \\
-- & 25\% & Final Presentation \& Report & 10-min presentation + 5-min Q\&A; comprehensive final report \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\noindent \textit{Note: In-Class Lesson Questions account for the remaining 5\%. A peer-review adjustment factor is applied to group grades at the end of the semester (not visible in Canvas). Use the Canvas-provided templates for every deliverable---do not create your own format.}
